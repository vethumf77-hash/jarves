JARVIS - VOICE ASSISTANT FOR YOUR WINDOWS PC
=============================================

START
  Open "Jarvis" from the Start menu or your Desktop.
  Wait until the globe turns blue (a few seconds), then:
    - say "Hey Jarvis", wait for "Yes?", and speak your command, or
    - press the Talk button, or
    - type a command in the box and press Enter.

EXAMPLES
  "open notepad"          "close spotify"          "take a screenshot"
  "set the volume to 30"  "mute"                    "set brightness to 60"
  "lock my PC"            "find my resume"          "search for weather in London"
  "start hand mouse"      (control the cursor with your hand in front of the webcam)

YOUR API KEY
  Jarvis uses the Groq API key you entered during setup.
  To change it later: open Jarvis > Settings > click Paste (or right-click > Paste) > Save.
  Free keys: https://console.groq.com/keys

TIPS
  - The "Mini" button shrinks Jarvis to a small floating globe in the corner.
  - Closing the window quits Jarvis.
  - Shut down / restart commands wait 30 seconds - say "cancel shutdown" to stop them.
  - Hand mouse needs a webcam; the hotkey Ctrl+Alt+H switches it on/off.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - Jarvis can't hear you? Open Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone and make sure
    "Let desktop apps access your microphone" is ON, and that the right microphone is the default.
    (Hand mouse needs the same permission under Camera.)
  - Make sure you have an internet connection and your microphone is the Windows default.
  - If it says your API key was rejected, open Settings and paste a fresh key.
  - Logs are in:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Jarvis\jarvis.log  (paste that path into File Explorer).

PRIVACY
  Nothing is sent anywhere until you say the wake word or press Talk. Only your spoken
  command is sent to Groq, using your own key. Your key is stored only on this PC.
  Uninstalling Jarvis (Settings > Apps) also deletes the saved key.
